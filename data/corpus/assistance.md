# Passenger Assist

Passenger Assist is a free service for anyone who needs help getting on or off a train, changing trains or moving through a station.

Passenger Assist should be booked at least 2 hours before your journey through the app, the website or by phone.

You can still turn up and go without booking, and staff will help you as soon as they can.

Assistance includes a ramp to board the train, help with luggage and being met at your destination station.

Assistance dogs are always welcome on trains and in stations free of charge.
