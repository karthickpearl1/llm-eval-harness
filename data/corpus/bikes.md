# Travelling with bicycles

Bicycles travel free of charge on all Pennine Rail services.

A bike reservation is required on Intercity services and can be made up to 2 hours before departure.

Each train carries a maximum of 2 full-size bicycles.

Folding bicycles can be taken on any service at any time without a reservation, as long as they are folded.

Electric scooters and e-unicycles are not permitted on trains or at stations.

Tandems and cargo bikes cannot be carried because they do not fit the cycle spaces.
