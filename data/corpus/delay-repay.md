# Delay Repay compensation

If your Pennine Rail journey arrives late, you can claim Delay Repay compensation regardless of the cause of the delay.

A delay of 15 to 29 minutes earns 25% of the single fare.

A delay of 30 to 59 minutes earns 50% of the single fare.

A delay of 60 to 119 minutes earns 100% of the single fare.

A delay of 120 minutes or more earns 100% of the return fare.

Claims must be submitted within 28 days of the delayed journey using the online Delay Repay form.

Compensation can be paid by bank transfer, card refund or rail travel vouchers.
