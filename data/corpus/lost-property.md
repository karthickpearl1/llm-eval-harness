# Lost property

Items left on Pennine Rail trains are taken to the lost property office at Leeds station.

Lost property is kept for 30 days before it is donated to charity or disposed of.

You can report a lost item using the online lost property form.

A handling fee of £5 is charged when you collect an item, and items can be posted for an additional delivery charge.

Lost bank cards and passports are handed to the issuing bank or to the police rather than being stored.
