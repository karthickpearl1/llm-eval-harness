# Railcards and discounts

The 16-25 Railcard gives one third off most fares and costs £35 for a year.

On weekdays before 10:00 a minimum fare of £12 applies when using the 16-25 Railcard, except during July and August.

The Senior Railcard is available to anyone aged 60 or over and also gives one third off most fares.

Children aged 5 to 15 travel at half the adult fare, and children under 5 travel free.

Railcard discounts cannot be applied to season tickets.
