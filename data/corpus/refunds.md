# Ticket refunds

Unused Advance tickets cannot be refunded, but they can be changed before departure for a £10 change fee plus any difference in fare.

Unused Anytime and Off-Peak tickets can be refunded if you apply within 28 days of the ticket expiry date.

An administration fee of £10 is deducted from each refund for Anytime and Off-Peak tickets.

No administration fee is charged when your train was cancelled or delayed and you chose not to travel.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment card within 10 working days of approval.

Season ticket holders can claim a partial refund for the unused portion of their ticket by returning it to a staffed ticket office.
